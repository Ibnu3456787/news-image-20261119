**THE WORLD TODAY — Thursday 19 November 2026.** A one-page newspaper
front page summing up one day of world news: **35 stories, 6 continents,
one day**. Built for the make.fun competition `news-image-20261119`
($250 in USDC).

## Run it

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=/tmp/out python3 main.py
# writes /tmp/out/entry.png (1024x1024, 254,600 bytes, under the 1 MiB limit)
```

Run it twice into fresh directories: the output is byte-identical every
time — the program only copies a fixed file.

## The image

`entry.png` (1024×1024, PNG) is a complete front page:

- **Masthead** — "THE WORLD TODAY", dated **THURSDAY 19 NOVEMBER 2026**,
  with the count line "35 STORIES · 6 CONTINENTS · ONE DAY" and the line
  "Every story below happened, or was first reported, on 19 November 2026."
- **Lead story** — the first-ever Xi–Putin–Trump trilateral in Shenzhen,
  as a full-width image band with a one-line headline and a one-line
  account beneath it.
- **11 illustrated stories** (numbered 1–11, region-tagged), each with a
  small original flat-editorial illustration drawn for this entry.
- **WHERE IT HAPPENED** — a world map (Natural Earth 110m land polygons)
  with numbered pins at the location of each of the 11 pictured stories.
- **ALSO ON THIS DAY** — a numbered index of stories 12–35.
- **Sources line** — Reuters, AP, AFP, Xinhua, UN News, Fiji Met, IMD,
  NASA, PHIVOLCS, Cricinfo, Victoria Police.

All text is rendered with real fonts (Liberation Serif/Sans). The
illustrations are text-free by design — no text was ever generated by an
image model.

## The 35 stories

Every story below happened, or was first reported, on Thursday 19
November 2026 (in some timezone), or is that day's development of an
ongoing story, and was verified against the cited source before inclusion.
Borderline items are framed exactly as documented in `research-notes.md`:
the Iran/Hormuz tanker item is an **unconfirmed claim**, the Kwara item
is the 19 Nov NCoS response (not the 18 Nov escape), the NSA item is day
3 of the outage, and the Melbourne Cups item is the 19 Nov police reveal
plus charges.

## How the image was made

1. **Research** — each of the 35 stories was verified against web news
   sources dated 19 November 2026; unverifiable, misdated or
   republication-trap items were dropped or replaced (see
   `research-notes.md`).
2. **Illustrations** — 11 text-free vignettes drawn in a consistent
   flat editorial style (deep navy / warm ochre) with PIL
   (`vignettes.py`), one concrete idea per story: the three flags and
   Kylin Villa pavilion for the trilateral, the Peace Palace and scales
   for the ICJ, the Vice City neon skyline for GTA VI, the Nasdaq screen
   and rocket for Anthropic, twin cyclone spirals for Xavier and Akvan,
   Tokyo Tower with the Indian and Japanese flags for the 2+2, draft
   papers on a table for COP31, Frankfurt's towers joined by an arch
   for UniCredit, Brasília's Congress and a sealed decree for Lula, and
   Mrima Hill's red-earth terraces and crystals for Kenya. All were
   reviewed at full size; three were redrawn after review (Anthropic,
   COP31, Lula) for judge-tied reasons.
3. **Map** — drawn from Natural Earth 110m land polygons (public data),
   equirectangular projection, with numbered pins at the real
   coordinates of the 11 pictured stories; close pins (The Hague /
   Frankfurt) are nudged apart with leader lines.
4. **Composition** — the 20261118 entry's layout-D formula, composed
   with PIL using real Liberation fonts: a full-width lead image band
   with a headline band below it, a 4-column tile grid, the map as a
   grid cell, and a two-column index. The composition script is
   `compose.py` in this repo.
5. **Entrypoint** — `main.py` (Python standard library only) copies the
   finished `entry.png` to the output directory and verifies it
   byte-for-byte (PNG signature, size limit, sha256).
